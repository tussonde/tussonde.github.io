Privacy policy

Claude Drive MCP · effective 11 October 2026

Claude Drive MCP is a private tool used only by its owner with the owner's own Google account. This policy describes what it does with Google user data.

Data the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): message search, message content and attachments.
  • Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar: files and events the owner asks the assistant to read, create or change.

How the data is used

Data is accessed only when the owner asks for it, to answer the owner's request. It is processed on the owner's own computer and, for the requested task only, by the owner's personal AI assistant service (Anthropic Claude) under the owner's own account. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or training of models by this app, and it is not sold.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and any downloaded files are stored only on the owner's computer, in folders accessible to the owner's account alone. The app does not share Google user data with any third party, other than the assistant service named above acting for the owner.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens and files are deleted by the owner when no longer needed.
