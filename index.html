Claude Drive MCP

A private, single-user tool. It is not offered to the public.

Claude Drive MCP connects its owner's personal AI assistant, running on the owner's own computer, to the owner's own Google account. At the owner's request it can:

  • search and read the owner's Gmail messages and download their attachments (read-only);
  • read and edit the owner's Google Drive files, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Calendar events.

Only the owner signs in to this app. It does not serve other users, has no public sign-up and shows no advertising.
